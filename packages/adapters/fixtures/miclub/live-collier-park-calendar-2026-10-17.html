Hey! Thanks for booking your round ⛳

Tee times open 8 days out at 12pm and need to be prepaid to lock in your spot. Concession/student rates are available—just bring your card when you arrive. Be sure to arrive 15 minutes before your tee time. Unlike your golf ball, we don't want your start time going missing!


You can also book carts and hire clubs here (each player needs a set, but we can sort you out onsite if needed). BYO wheels? Check your permit first! Scooters (ride-on or stand-up), e-bikes and electric ride-on buggies are a no-go unless permitted by the City of South Perth, with the permit clearly displayed on the vehicle. No permit, no play! Anyone using an unapproved vehicle will be asked to leave the premises, with no refund provided.

﻿

Plans changed? No worries—cancel 24+ hours ahead and we’ll issue a credit. Late cancellations or no-shows will forfeit the fee (as per T&Cs).
