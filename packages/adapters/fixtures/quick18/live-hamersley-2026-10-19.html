As part of our regular course maintenance program done year, we have cored and sanded the greens, fairways, and tees to ensure the long-term health and quality of the course.

These essential works may result in temporary playing surfaces that are not at their usual standard while the course recovers. We appreciate your understanding that recovery is a natural part of the maintenance process.

The course reopens for play as of this Saturday 17th October, and by choosing to play during this recovery period, golfers acknowledge and accept the temporary conditions.

We thank you for your patience and support as we undertake these important improvements for the benefit of all players.

We are currently reviewing and updating all hole indexes to reflect recent layout changes:
• 10th Hole is now a Par 3
• 18th Hole is now a Par 5
If you use a digital scorecard, please contact MiClub to ensure your details are updated correctly.

About the Course Stirling Leisure’s Hamersley Public Golf Course is a picturesque 18 hole venue set amongst lush parkland, just 20 minutes from the Perth CBD. The course offers a fair challenge for golfers of all abilities, with tree lined holes and fairways and greens maintained in excellent condition. We offer:
• Electric golf carts, including EZ GO
• Pull trolleys
• State-of-the-art 30 bay driving range
Everything is designed to elevate your golfing experience from the moment you arrive.

Online Bookings
Tee times are released daily at 7.00pm for the following 14 days. Players may book:
• Up to five tee-time slots in one transaction
• A maximum of 20 players per booking
• Walk-ins are welcome, subject to availability.

For general enquiries, please email us EMAIL US..

Before booking, please ensure you review our CONDITIONS OF PLAY.

A Reminder to Our Guests
When you arrive onsite, please treat our staff with kindness and respect. We appreciate your support in helping us maintain a welcoming environment for everyone.
